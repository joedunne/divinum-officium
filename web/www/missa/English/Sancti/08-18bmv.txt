Fourth day of the octave of the Assumption of Blessed Mary;;Semiduplex;;2;;ex Sancti/08-15

[Oratio]
O Lord, we beseech thee, forgive the transgressions of thy servants, and, forasmuch as by our own deeds we cannot please thee, may we find safety through the prayers of the Mother of thy Son and our Lord.
$Qui tecum

[Secreta]
May the prayer of the Mother of God assist Thy people, O Lord: that although we know she has passed away according to the condition of the flesh, we may feel her interceding for us with Thee in heavenly glory.
$Per eundem

[Postcommunio]
Having been made partakers of the heavenly table, we implore Thy clemency, O Lord our God: that we who honor the Assumption of the Mother of God may, by her intercession, be delivered from all looming evils.
$Per eundem
