[Officium]
St. Isidore the Farmer, Confessor

[Oratio]
Grant us, we beseech Thee, merciful God, through the intercession of blessed Isidore the Farmer, Thy Confessor, not to savor of pride; but by his merits and examples, to serve Thee always with a humility pleasing to Thee.
